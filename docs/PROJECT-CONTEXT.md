# The Scenes — Canonical Project Context

## Product

The Scenes is an African-first, globally expandable learning, teaching, community, discovery, and creator platform.

It combines:
- learning;
- teaching;
- focused communities;
- discovery;
- courses and learning paths;
- projects and challenges;
- live sessions/events;
- resources;
- workflows/templates;
- creator profiles;
- creator monetization;
- later, marketplace and AI-assisted capabilities.

## AI

AI is a flagship category and major capability, not the company boundary.

The architecture must support:
- AI and automation;
- technology and coding;
- web/product;
- design and creative work;
- content;
- business/freelancing;
- marketing/sales;
- career/professional skills;
- communication/soft skills;
- education/languages;
- industry-specific learning;
- other legitimate specialist knowledge.

## African-first

The product should feel global in quality but not globally generic.

Design for:
- mobile-first use;
- bandwidth sensitivity;
- local trust;
- direct channels such as WhatsApp;
- local payment patterns when payments are introduced;
- creators already operating through social platforms and direct communities;
- international users on the same product.

## Competitive shape

Use proven patterns from Skool, Circle, Kajabi, and related products where useful.

Do not build a one-to-one clone.

The Scenes should differentiate through:
- structured discovery;
- multiple learning/teaching modes;
- creator tooling;
- practical outcomes;
- community + learning integration;
- future workflow/knowledge systems;
- African-first operating considerations.

## Product surfaces

Public:
Landing, Explore, Categories, Communities, Courses, Teachers/Creators, Resources/Workflows, Pricing, Become a Teacher, About, Help, Legal.

Authenticated:
Home, Explore, My Learning, Community, Workflows, Resources, Events, Marketplace later, Notifications, Profile.

Teacher/Creator:
Overview, Content, Courses, Lessons, Live Sessions, Products, Community, Students, Reviews, Earnings, Analytics, Settings.

Admin:
Overview, Users, Teachers/Creators, Categories, Content, Reports, Payments, Payouts, Moderation, Policies, Analytics, System Settings.

## Landing vs discovery

Landing is marketing and acquisition.

Authenticated Home/Explore is product discovery and activity.

Never merge these concepts accidentally.

## UX target

Mobile is a primary design target.

The system should feel:
- credible;
- editorial;
- calm;
- practical;
- human;
- accessible.

Avoid generic AI-dashboard aesthetics.

## Current launch posture

Build the frontend first with deterministic fixtures.

Do not connect production auth/database/payment/provider services until the design + frontend gates are passed.
